## QL on Mac 0.1.0

The first version. One window sets Quake Live up on an Apple silicon Mac and starts it through Steam.

### What it installs
QL on Mac downloads its own Wine engine, athei's Mach9 0.4.0, and checks it against its checksum. It
adds glmt, my OpenGL to Metal translator, and three Wine libraries built with my patches. Then it
makes a Wine bottle, runs Valve's Steam installer in it and opens Steam on Quake Live's page. You
sign in and install the game there. Steam and the game come from Valve.

Everything goes into `~/Library/Application Support/QL on Mac`, about 5 GB with the game. More,
then Uninstall, removes it.

### Play
Play starts Steam and then the game, full screen at your display's own resolution, with vsync off.
The button says how far the start is while Steam comes up, signs in and launches the game. Once
Steam remembers your login it starts without its window.

On my M5 Max the game holds its cap of 250 fps at 3840x2160.

### Settings
- Frame cap, 125 or 250. The game saves it, and Steam Cloud carries it to your other computers.
- Retina, on or off. Off starts the game at the display's size in points. I have not played with it off.
- Steam in the background.
- Quit Steam with the game.
- Automatic updates of the app.

### What you need
An Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26.5 or later, and Quake Live on your Steam account.

### Known problems
- 250 frames a second don't divide into 60, so the picture tears on a 60 Hz display.
- The game's menu is a web page drawn in software. A click there can take a second to show. In a
  match nothing of that is left.
- `r_gamma`, bloom and colour correction do nothing. glmt has no shaders.
- I have run this on one Mac, mine, on macOS 27. Tell me what happens on yours in the
  [issues](https://github.com/jdrly/ql-on-mac/issues).
